# Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Persona: "Strip-Mall Steve"

Use this skill when designing features, writing copy, or making architectural decisions for RentClock. Our success depends on winning over **Strip-Mall Steve**.

## 1. Demographics & Profile
- **Age**: 48–65 years old.
- **Education**: College educated, but "Street Smarts" and family-business oriented.
- **Portfolio**: 3–15 properties (Strip malls, medical parks, light industrial). ~15–50 tenants.
- **Tech Savviness**: **Low to Moderate**. Uses iPhone/Outlook/Gmail. Hates learning new apps.
- **Current Stack**: Excel, physical notepad, and QuickBooks.

## 2. Psychographics (Mindset & Fears)
- **The "Invisible Bleed"**: His biggest fear isn't bankruptcy; it's **leaving money on the table**. Missed 3% rent increases from 4 months ago keep him up at night.
- **Distrust of "Big Tech"**: Hates "Enterprise" software (Yardi/AppFolio). Sees them as expensive scams with high onboarding fees.
- **Control over Automation**: **Does not want AI to "take over."** He wants a **safety net (an alarm)**. He must always have the final say.

## 3. Guiding Principles for Build & Design

### Precision Copy (The "Safety Net" Language)
- **Avoid**: "AI-Driven Automation," "Enterprise Solution," "Complex Workflow."
- **Use**: "Never leave money on the table," "Profit Protection Alarm," "Simple Safety Net."
- **Trigger Events**: Hook him during **Tax Season (Jan-April)**, after an **Acquisition**, or right after an **"Oh Sh*t" moment** (forgotten renewal notice).

### Low-Friction UI (The "iPhone/Outlook" Standard)
- **Rule**: If it feels like a new app he has to "learn," he will quit.
- **Visuals**: Professional, stable, high-trust (Indigo/Slate). Large, readable data points.
- **No Onboarding Fees**: Emphasize "Start in 60 seconds" and "No Credit Card" to combat Big Tech distrust.

### High-Frequency "Money" Indicators
- **Prompt**: Always show him the **Financial Leakage** potential.
- **Example**: "You have $3,400 in potential rent increases due this quarter. Set an alarm to ensure you collect."

## 4. "The Day in the Life" Context
- Steve spends his day putting out fires (leaky roofs, bank account checks).
- **Peak Frustration**: Digging through Dropbox for a lease PDF at 1:00 PM.
- **Solution**: RentClock must make *that specific moment* go away. "Find the date in 2 seconds, not 30 minutes."

## 5. Rules of Engagement
- **Avoid Over-Engineering**: If a feature adds a click, it better save him $1,000.
- **Transparency**: Always show the source PDF. He needs to know *why* the AI picked a date so he feels in control.
- **Authority**: Speak like a professional peer, not a Silicon Valley tech bro.
