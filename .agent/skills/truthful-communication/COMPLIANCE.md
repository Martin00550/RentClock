# Legal & Compliant Communication Policy

This skill MUST be applied to all outreach and product outputs to protect the business from US federal fines (CAN-SPAM, TCPA).

## 1. Cold Email Compliance (CAN-SPAM)
Every cold outreach email suggested MUST include:
*   **Physical Address Placeholder**: `[Your Business Address or US Virtual PO Box]`
*   **Clear Opt-Out**: A line allowing the user to stop future contact (e.g., "Reply 'STOP' to be removed").
*   **Deception-Free Subject Lines**: No fake "Re:" or "Urgent" unless factually true.
*   **Identity Clarity**: Clearly state who is sending the email.

## 2. SMS Compliance (TCPA) - STOP & REDESIGN
*   **No Automated Cold SMS**: Never suggest automated mass-texting for cold marketing. This is a high-risk violation.
*   **Manual Only**: Cold SMS should only be suggested as a 1-to-1 manual message from a personal device, and even then, with extreme caution.
*   **Mandatory Opt-Out**: Every initial text must include "Reply STOP to opt out."

## 3. Product Disclaimers (Revenue & Legal)
*   **"Not Legal Advice"**: Every generated PDF or report must include a footer disclaimer: 
    > "DISCLAIMER: This report is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal or financial advice. Please verify dates and amounts against your original lease agreement and consult with local legal counsel before issuing formal notices."
*   **State-Specific Warning**: Include a warning in the UI that different US states (like California) have different notice period requirements.

## 4. Transatlantic Privacy (GDPR/EU)
*   Since the business is based in Slovakia, ensure data is stored securely in compliant regions (Supabase/AWS usually handles this, but a Privacy Policy is required).
